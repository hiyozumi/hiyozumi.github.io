# Hello, I goes by the name zumi

Welcome to my little corner of the internet.

I'm an aspiring software engineer who enjoys bringing ideas into existence. And I enjoy exploring new things as well. I don't really have a particular theme in mind for this space. Think of it as a personal notebook that happens to be available on the internet.

## What I'm up to

Here's a few things currently occupying my time:

- **Software Engineering:** Exploring software development, backend engineering, and different technologies. I enjoy building things and figuring out how they work.
- **Japanese:** Studying Japanese and gradually working towards becoming comfortable using it in everyday life.
- **Game Development:** Learning about game design, programming, and the creative process behind making games.
- **Creative Works:** Experimenting with digital art and music production, mostly for personal enjoyment.

I'm still learning in most of these areas, but there's always something new to explore.

## Things I enjoy

Outside of software development, I have quite a few interests.

- **Games:** JRPGs, MMORPGs, tabletop RPGs, and board games. I particularly enjoy games with interesting worlds, storytelling, and meaningful player choices.
- **Art:** Drawing characters, exploring different art styles, and occasionally making illustrations.
- **Music:** Listening to orchestral, game, and anime soundtracks. Recently, I've also been exploring music production.
- **Reading:** Novels, manga, and whatever happens to catch my attention.
- **Languages:** Learning Japanese, discovering new expressions, and occasionally getting lost in the nuances of the language.

I also enjoy sharing these hobbies with other people, whether that's playing a tabletop campaign, discussing games, or simply exchanging ideas.
