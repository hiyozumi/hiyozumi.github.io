# Hello, I goes by the name ズミ a.k.a. zumi 

I'm an aspiring software engineer who writes, may it be a piece of code or storytelling, and sometimes do artworks. I have interest in software development, illustration, music, and world building. I enjoy bringing ideas into existence wheter through software or creative works.

## Here's a few things currently occupying my time,

- **Software Engineering:** Exploring software development, backend engineering, and different technologies. I enjoy building things and putting things together into one working system is fascinating.
- **Japanese:** Studying Japanese and gradually working towards becoming comfortable using it in everyday life.
- **Game Dev:** Figuring out about game design, programming, and the creative process behind making games.
- **Creative Works:** Getting my hand into digital art and music production, mostly for personal exploration.

I'm still learning in most of these areas, and that's the good thing as there's always something new to me.

## Things I enjoy

Outside of software development, I have quite a few interests.

- **Games:** JRPGs, MMORPGs, and board games. I particularly enjoy games with interesting worlds and storytelling, especially with player choice that affect the rest of the playthrough. Though sometimes, I played party game when we got a lot of people playing.
- **Music:** Have you ever alone in a room, with some orchestral music in the background playing through your ears, and immersing yourself into what kinda situation you are in between each music. That's what made me like this thing.
- **Japanese Culture:** What I really enjoy since high school, and recently it becomes part of my Japanese learning as well. I've read a lot of Japanese novel and manga, and with a wide variety of genre, tho I tend to read psychological genre mostly.
- **TTRPG:** There's almost a weekly session I joined, and I enjoyed a lot sharing my time with my table, playing as our character and living up the world our DM shared to us, It's a great escape for the weekend.
