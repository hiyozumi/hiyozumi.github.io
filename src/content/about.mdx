# Hello, I goes by the name zumi 

Welcome to my little corner on the internet. I'm an aspiring software engineer who enjoys bringing ideas into existence. And I enjoy exploring new things as well. I don't really have a particular theme in mind for this space. Think of it as a personal notebook that happens to be available on the internet.

## Here's a few things currently occupying my time,

- **Software Engineering:** Exploring software development, backend engineering, and different technologies. I enjoy building things and figuring out how they work.
- **Japanese:** Studying Japanese and gradually working towards becoming comfortable using it in everyday life.
- **Game Dev:** Trying out about game design, programming, and the creative process behind making games.
- **Creative Works:** Experimenting with digital art and music production, mostly for personal enjoyment.

I'm still learning in most of these areas, and that's the good thing as there's always something new to explore.

## Things I enjoy

Outside of software development, I have quite a few interests.

- **Games:** JRPGs, MMORPGs, tabletop RPGs, and board games. I particularly enjoy games with interesting worlds, storytelling, and player choices that affects the course of the game.
- **Art:** For now it's only drawing characters, but at some point being able to draw concept of something you have an ideas on is what I'm looking forward.
- **Music:** Listening to orchestral, game, and anime soundtracks. Recently, I've also been exploring music composition.
- **Japanese Culture:** I enjoy this recently, as part of my Japanese learning as well. I've read a lot of Japanese novel and manga with great world building.
- **TTRPG:** There's almost a weekly session I joined, and I enjoyed a lot sharing my time with my table, playing as our character and living up the world our DM shared to us, It's a great escape for the weekend.
